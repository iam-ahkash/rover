🎯 Calibrate sensors

Do the steps in order once for each phone and mount. Everything is saved on the phone.

1 · Tilt sensor & gyroscope

Lay the phone flat, screen up, on a table. Tap, and don't touch it for 3 s. Then turn it around (top where the bottom was) and tap again. This removes the sensor's own error, even if the table isn't level.

2 · Camera height & distance

  1. Mount the phone as it will be used and set its height above the floor.
  2. Put something on the floor at a measured distance (tape measure) and enter it.
  3. Tap the picture exactly where it touches the floor.
Not calibrated yet.

3 · Lens (field of view) — optional

The lens value is already known for this phone. For another phone: stand a flat object of known width (a door, a sheet of A4 = 0.21 m) facing the camera at the distance above, then tap its left and right edges.
